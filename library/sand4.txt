It breathes where shadows learn to crawl,
behind the eyes, beneath it all.
It waits for cracks in tempered glass,
for moments when the strong collapse.


It feeds on calm, on quiet air,
on whispered doubts and soft despair.
Each fear becomes its favorite meat,
each secret makes the pulse repeat.


It speaks in tones that echo pain,
a choir of loss, a low refrain.
It builds its throne from sleepless nights,
from guilt that stings, from dying lights.


It wears a grin that mocks the pure,
a sickness dressed to look demure.
It grows when kindness starts to fade,
it blooms where hope was once displayed.


No blade can pierce, no prayer can win,
no saint can cleanse what rots within.
It waits, it learns, it always knows—
the deeper darkness softly grows.


And when the final breath is thin,
the last of light consumed by sin,
the world will see what hid in skin—
not man, nor soul—the monster within.
