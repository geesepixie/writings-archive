A ghost among the clocks,
she wanders the hours no one claims.
Each tick stretches like a corridor,
each chime a door that stays closed.

The minutes pass around her,
empty and still,
their passage unnoticed,
never reaching her.

Time itself feels deserted—
a house surrendered to silence,
its mirrors holding only
the faint trace of her presence.

She drifts through these rooms,
poised and careful,
as fragile and fleeting as the breath
that fogs a glass and disappears.

The world moves on elsewhere,
but here—
in the company of clocks—
she tends to what has been left behind,
and what is left behind tends to her.
