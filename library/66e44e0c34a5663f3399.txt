And so she rose, the honeybee,
Anointed by the morning’s flame,
Beneath the vault of sapphire sea,
The heavens whispered forth her name.

The meadows bent to hear her flight,
The blossoms bowed as choirs would pray;
Her song, a fountain robed in light,
Made night recede and crowned the day.

O harp, O lyre, resound her breath,
Exalt the soul no grave could bind;
Through time’s decay, through mortal death,
Her stream of music winds and winds.

Let deserts, parched by ages’ fire,
Unseal their lips to taste her dream;
The barren stone, the ashen mire,
Revive beneath her living stream.

So moves she, crowned in golden wings,
Through mortal dust, through mortal years;
A flame that neither silence stills,
A song that sanctifies our tears.

And when the stars to embers fall,
When oceans fade, when suns decline,
Her voice shall be the final call,
The honeyed echo still sublime.
