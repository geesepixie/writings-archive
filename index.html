“A writer is a world trapped in a person”

Writing has become my way of tracing the subtle currents of thought, of following how ideas unfold, and of preserving fleeting impressions that might otherwise vanish. Each reflection is a companion, charting the evolution of thought and feeling.

I seldom give my pieces titles; a single phrase can never contain the whole. What appears as a title is merely a window into the text, a hint rather than a definition. The true meaning resides in the words—and in the spaces between them, where reflection lingers.

So linger if you wish. Take what resonates, leave the rest. Here you will find observations, personal reflections, occasional poems, and perhaps a layer glimpsed only by those who stay long enough. Wander slowly, and enjoy your stay among words that were never meant to be shared.
